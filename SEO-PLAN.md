Its own website could not be retrieved by the research tool. No technical ranking comparison was possible.
- The fetched public homepage still shows the old design. Local changes have not been published. Search results from this research are not a location-controlled Google ranking report; review totals and Google Maps positions were not verified.
- Our verified differentiator: a qualified groomer, home salon, and no other dogs present during the appointment.

## Implemented in the local checkout

Descriptive local page titles and homepage heading; original service summaries; visible booking questions and answers; contextual links; canonical URLs; XML sitemap; robots.txt; LocalBusiness and service catalog JSON-LD; consistent address and phone; responsive layouts; semantic HTML and no client-side rendering dependency. The contact map loads lazily. Existing page paths and photos remain available.

## Priority 1 — publish and establish a baseline

1. Publish this checkout through the existing GitHub Pages workflow. Confirm the custom domain and Enforce HTTPS setting. Test the root URL, all six HTML pages, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Confirm HTTP redirects to HTTPS and www redirects to the chosen non-www domain. These are hosting checks, not claims that redirects are already configured.
2. Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console. Submit https://trimsalonbarendrecht.nl/sitemap.xml . Inspect the homepage and treatments page; check indexing eligibility and Google's selected canonical. Request indexing after publication.
3. Record a baseline of non-brand impressions, clicks, queries and contact conversions. Track “trimsalon Barendrecht”, “hondentrimsalon Barendrecht” and relevant treatment queries. Compare rolling 28-day periods, accounting for seasonality; average position alone is not a booking metric.
4. Run PageSpeed Insights on the deployed homepage and contact page, on mobile. Inspect layout shifts and image loading before selecting further performance work. Browser screenshots and field Core Web Vitals have not been verified in this environment.

## Priority 2 — Google Business Profile

- Verify and complete the existing profile. Keep the real salon name, address, phone and website consistent with the site. Choose the most accurate available dog-grooming category and list only services actually offered.
- Maintain appointment-only information, normal hours and special closure dates. Confirm the year of the November/December closure, then update the website notice and applicable structured special hours. Remove expired notices.
- Upload current, authentic salon and grooming photos with owner consent where appropriate.
- Ask customers neutrally for an honest Google review after appointments. No incentives or filtering for positive reviewers. Reply helpfully to feedback. Use the official review link from the profile; do not guess it.
- Check Business Profile performance for calls and website clicks alongside Search Console data.

## Priority 3 — evidence and local reputation

- Add the actual qualification name, experience, current prices/pricing basis and new-customer availability once the owner confirms them.
- Keep verified listings consistent, starting with existing local and grooming directories. Correct errors; avoid bulk paid links.
- Seek legitimate mentions through existing local relationships, such as a local business association or an actual newsworthy salon story. Do not claim partnerships that do not exist.
- Add fresh grooming examples and useful answers based on genuine customer questions. Avoid copying competitors or creating pages only to repeat keywords.

## Limits and references

Google says local visibility depends on relevance, distance and prominence, including links and reviews. Website changes cannot guarantee outranking a particular competitor or overcome every searcher's distance: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 .

AI search uses the same fundamental crawlability and content foundations; special AI text files are not required for Google's AI features: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features .

The sitemap aids discovery but does not guarantee indexing. No access to Search Console, Business Profile, backlink data or verified local rank tracking was available during this work.
